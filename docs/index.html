Max Player

A local video library & player for Android.

Max Player plays the videos already on your device - mp4, webm, mkv, avi and more - with subtitles, audio-track switching, A-B loop, sleep timer, playback speed up to 3x, karaoke word highlight, dialogue boost, picture enhancement, private folder, network (SMB) playback and optional Google Drive streaming. Local-first: your files never leave your phone unless you explicitly stream them from your own Drive.
