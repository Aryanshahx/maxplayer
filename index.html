Max Player

Welcome to Max Player, a video playback application designed to let users stream and watch their personal video collections stored in their own Google Drive.

Key Features

  • Securely sign in with your Google account.
  • Browse and stream your personal video files directly from Google Drive.
  • Smooth, high-performance media playback.
