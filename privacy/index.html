RunX Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026
Developer: Hyper Tech Labs (Aryan Shah)
Contact: techlabs.hyper@gmail.com

Overview
RunX Player is a media player for local files and optional online content. This version does not display advertisements and does not include the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It still uses usage analytics, diagnostic reporting and push-notification services. These services send the data described below off your device. We do not sell personal data.

Media, permissions and local settings
Media permissions let you select, discover and play files and save screenshots. Your local media library, playlists, playback history, private-folder settings, selected interface language and appearance preference (including custom RGB accent values) are stored on your device. Search fields keep up to three recent queries per search category locally for convenient reuse; you can clear them from the Recent searches section. Private Space PINs, connection credentials and stream URL fields are not included in recent-search history. They are not uploaded by our analytics or notification features. Private-folder protection uses local storage and device authentication; it is not a cloud backup or a guarantee of file encryption. Saved Network Storage hosts, paths, usernames and passwords are stored in app-local preferences, not the VPN Keystore-backed encrypted store. Use restricted accounts and trusted networks; remove saved locations to delete these connection entries. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove settings and app-private files.

Microphone access is used when you choose voice search. Voice recognition is provided by Android's installed recognition service; whether recognition is processed locally or online depends on that service and your device settings. On-device AI subtitle generation processes media locally after any required model download. Interface translations are bundled and work offline; changing language does not send your text to a translation service.

Usage information and diagnostics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase database services to understand app use, maintain reliability and investigate failures. Information includes app opens, feature interactions, playback counts, session duration, time-of-day buckets, app/device information and pseudonymous installation identifiers. Firebase Analytics may derive approximate geographic information from network connections.

Error reports may include exception text, stack traces, timestamps, app version, a report identifier, failure category and a failing stream's hostname. Full stream URLs, media filenames, library contents, search terms and passwords are not intentionally included in these usage counters. Technical error text may nevertheless contain contextual information. Some reports and events are queued locally and sent when connectivity returns. Not all native crashes or interruptions can be captured.

Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a pseudonymous installation user ID; this is not a verified personal identity. Clearing app data or reinstalling can change identifiers. There is no advertising SDK or in-app advertising. Advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization signals are disabled; installation and notification identifiers are still used for the purposes described here.

Approximate location
The app contacts https://api.country.is/ to estimate the connection's country. That provider receives your IP address. The app extracts a two-letter country code for aggregate geographic usage statistics and does not store or forward the returned IP field to our database. The lookup may be retried if unsuccessful. VPNs, proxies and network routing can affect the result. This feature does not request GPS coordinates, city or address, and is not continuous location monitoring. Provider information: https://country.is/

Notifications
With your notification permission, Firebase Cloud Messaging uses installation identifiers and push tokens to deliver general or installation-specific app notices. You can block notifications in Android settings. Notification images may be fetched from HTTPS hosts, including GitHub; those providers receive the network connection. Selecting a notice or link may open relevant app content or an external website. Delivery is not guaranteed.

Optional online features and sharing
Movie discovery and search contact The Movie Database (TMDB). Optional online AI recommendations send the descriptions/questions you submit to OpenRouter and its selected model provider. Do not enter confidential information. These providers receive requests and connection information and handle them under their policies. Local media is not automatically uploaded for online recommendations.

Network playback contacts the stream hosts you choose. Trailers and external websites contact their respective providers. There are no promotional partner banners or affiliate buttons. Where an Android file-picker import is offered, your installed storage provider handles access and may require its own sign-in. The main quick tile opens VPN, not a cloud sign-in service. Quick Share transfers selected files to the recipient you choose over the network. Anyone with access to a live sharing link may be able to download those selected files; stop sharing when finished. Local-network sharing and user-supplied non-HTTPS media sources may not be encrypted.

Optional VPN
The VPN is off by default and starts only after you tap Connect. Android requests VPN approval when required; there is no separate in-app consent or instruction screen. RunX Player only routing is the default; you can explicitly choose full-device routing. The app uses WireGuard or OpenVPN. It does not operate VPN exit servers. WARP registration sends a generated public key, generic Android/client/locale information and a terms-acceptance timestamp to Cloudflare; its application terms are available at https://www.cloudflare.com/application/terms/. Cloudflare WARP registration, Riseup provisioning and VPN Gate server-directory requests contact those providers; volunteer/custom relay operators receive routed traffic. These providers receive your IP address and connection metadata, can observe destination addresses and unencrypted traffic, and apply their own privacy, logging and retention policies. A VPN is not a guarantee of anonymity, speed, country selection or availability.

The app checks the exit IP/country using https://1.1.1.1/cdn-cgi/trace after a connection. Exit information and byte counters are displayed locally, not intentionally sent to our analytics. Custom VPN configurations and private keys are stored locally with Android Keystore-backed encryption in no-backup storage where supported; if secure storage is unavailable they remain session-only. VPN profile contents are not intentionally sent to our analytics or crash reporting. Provider-generated Riseup credentials are obtained from Riseup and encrypted locally. Remove a custom profile to delete it locally, or clear app data to remove app settings and stored VPN profiles. Selected profile and routing preferences are saved locally. Only one Android VPN can be active; connecting can replace an existing VPN. There is no in-app kill switch; normal connectivity can resume after disconnection unless separately blocked in Android settings. An ongoing VPN notification/system indicator may be shown.

Provider policies and terms:
- Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
- Riseup VPN: https://riseup.net/en/vpn
- VPN Gate: https://www.vpngate.net/en/about_us.aspx
Custom VPN providers and volunteer operators may have separate policies. Review and trust the selected operator before routing sensitive traffic.

Reviews
After sufficient use, the app may ask Google Play to display its native review dialog. Google controls availability and frequency. Local eligibility counters and the last request time are stored on your device. The app does not receive your rating or review text from this prompt. Reviews you submit to Google Play are handled and may be published by Google under its policies.

Providers and security
Providers include Google/Firebase, country.is and, when used, TMDB, OpenRouter/model providers, storage providers, recipients and stream or website hosts. Firebase data is transmitted using its secure transport. No system can guarantee absolute security; use trusted networks and sources, especially for unencrypted streams or local sharing.

Provider information:
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- TMDB: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

Retention, choices and requests
Appearance, interface language and custom accent changes stay local; changing these does not disable online services. Deny media or microphone access in Android settings to restrict those features, stop Quick Share to close its active links, and disconnect VPN to stop the tunnel. Disconnecting does not delete records already held by network providers. Removing downloaded AI models or cached movie artwork is separate from deleting provider-side information.

Local settings and cached information remain until removed through app/device controls or uninstalling, subject to Android backup behaviour. Usage statistics, installation/token records and diagnostic reports are retained as needed for the purposes above; this app does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period. Some statistics are aggregate and cannot be linked back to an individual. Blocking notifications does not by itself erase existing installation records or disable analytics. There is currently no in-app opt-out for usage/diagnostic collection.

For access, deletion or other privacy requests, email techlabs.hyper@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or private media. We may need information to locate a pseudonymous record and verify a request; some aggregate information cannot be attributed to you. We assess requests under applicable law. Removing local data alone does not erase records already sent to online services.

Children and changes
The app is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We may revise this policy when the app's practices change. The current policy is available in the app and at the public policy URL. Previously installed older apps may have different practices; review the policy for the app you use.
